<h2>Exploring Earth's interior<br>through seismic waves.</h2>
<p class="hero-lead">I study what seismic waveforms can tell us about the structure and physical properties of Earth's interior.</p>
</div>

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Geophysics at the Colorado School of Mines, working with [Ebru Bozdağ](https://geophysics.mines.edu/project/bozdag-ebru/). My research combines numerical wave propagation, seismic measurements, and inverse methods to investigate Earth's mantle and the effects of complex structure on seismic waves.

I am interested in using information beyond arrival times, including wave amplitudes, attenuation, and Rayleigh-wave ellipticity, to improve how we image the Earth. I also work on earthquake source inversion and the interpretation of body-wave phases and SS precursors.

## A little about me

Before coming to Mines, I completed my BS–MS in Geological Sciences at IISER Kolkata, where I worked on seismic attenuation in the Himalaya. My earlier research also involved earthquake detection and relocation in Jammu and Kashmir. I speak Bengali, English, and Hindi.
