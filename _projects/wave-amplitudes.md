## The question

Traveltimes are widely used to image the mantle, but seismic amplitudes carry additional information about the structures encountered by a wave. Can this information help us choose how strongly to regularise a traveltime inversion?

## My approach

I compare differential traveltimes and amplitude ratios for S, SS, and Sdiff using observations and synthetic waveforms computed in 1D and 3D Earth models. The comparisons examine how well mantle models reproduce both measurements, and how amplitude information can help assess model complexity.

## What this tells us

The study evaluates amplitudes as complementary constraints on traveltime tomography. It also examines the difficulty of reproducing Sdiff amplitudes and the possible contribution of lowermost-mantle structure.
