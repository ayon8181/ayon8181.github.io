## The question

What can small, precisely located earthquakes reveal about active structures beneath the northwestern Himalaya?

## My approach

My earlier work involved earthquake detection and relocation using records from the Jammu and Kashmir Seismological Network. I worked with detection methods including template matching to improve the identification of small events.
