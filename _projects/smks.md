## The question

SKS and SKKS sample the mantle and outer core. Their differential traveltimes are often interpreted after applying ray-theoretical mantle corrections. How well do these corrections reproduce measurements from full waveforms?

## My approach

I compare ray-theoretical predictions with cross-correlation traveltimes measured from spectral-element synthetics. Tests with different mantle and crustal models separate the effects of large-scale mantle structure, crustal complexity, and finite-frequency wave propagation. I also compare synthetic measurements with observations and examine sensitivity to outer-core reference structure.

## Why it matters

Mantle and crustal contributions can affect how we interpret residuals attributed to the outer core. This project assesses where ray corrections capture the waveform measurements and where more complete modelling becomes important.
