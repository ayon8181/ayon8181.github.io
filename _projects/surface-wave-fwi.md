## Research focus

During my 2026 research internship at TGS in Houston, I worked on surface-wave full-waveform inversion, exploring the behaviour of envelope and waveform misfits in numerical experiments.

## Methods

The work involved seismic waveform processing, adjoint-source development, and the organisation of repeated inversion experiments.
