## The question

S and ScS follow different mantle paths. Can differences in their waveform frequency content provide useful measurements of path-integrated attenuation?

## My approach

I am exploring differential attenuation measurements using synthetic seismograms, including instantaneous-frequency approaches. Tests begin with a 1D reference Earth before considering the effects of 3D structure.

## Current directions

The emphasis is on validating the measurements and understanding the assumptions needed to interpret differential t-star estimates.
