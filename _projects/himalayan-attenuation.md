## The question

How does seismic attenuation vary across the northwestern Himalaya and western Tibet?

## My approach

During my BS–MS research at IISER Kolkata, I analysed Lg-coda waves to investigate regional attenuation and its frequency dependence. The work used regional seismic records, including the Jammu and Kashmir Seismological Network.

## Background

This research formed part of my thesis work with Supriyo Mitra and contributed to my interest in seismic attenuation and Earth imaging.
