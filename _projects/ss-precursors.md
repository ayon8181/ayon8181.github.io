## The question

SS precursors provide information about mantle discontinuities. How do 3D structure and waveform interference affect the signals we recover through stacking?

## My approach

I compare spectral-element synthetics in PREM, S40RTS, and GLAD-M35 using distance sections and time–slowness vespagrams. Global and regional stacks help examine coherence, timing, and waveform differences near the expected precursor arrivals.

## Current directions

Regional tests include the Pacific, continental United States, and the Himalayan region. I am investigating the effects of source depth, record coverage, SS alignment, and the different reference discontinuity depths used by the models.
