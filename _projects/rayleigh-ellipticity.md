## The question

The relative strength of radial and vertical Rayleigh-wave motion depends on Earth structure. How can we use this ellipticity information in a waveform inversion?

## My approach

I develop measurements based on radial-to-vertical amplitude ratios and derive the corresponding adjoint sources and sensitivity kernels. Tests in a 1D reference model examine measurement behaviour and kernel sensitivity, with applications towards 3D crustal imaging.

## Current directions

I am exploring how measurement choices affect sensitivity and how multiple event azimuths contribute to a combined structural gradient. A regional application focuses on the continental United States.

## Context

This work was presented at IAMG 2026. A journal manuscript is in preparation.
